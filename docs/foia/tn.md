**Subject:** Public records request — historical WARN Act notices (voluntary release)

Dear Public Records Request Coordinator,

I request copies of all electronically available records of Worker Adjustment and
Retraining Notification (WARN) Act notices received by the Department's Dislocated
Worker Unit, from the earliest electronically available date through December 31,
2024. The Department already publishes identical records for 2021 onward on its
public WARN reports page, so the older listings sought here are plainly
non-confidential. A complete export through the present is equally welcome if that
is easier to produce.

I am aware that T.C.A. §10-7-503 reserves the right of access to Tennessee
citizens and that I am writing from out of state; nothing in the Act, however,
prohibits the Department from releasing these records voluntarily, and I
respectfully ask that it do so in the same public-interest spirit in which it
already publishes the recent years.

I am making this request on my own behalf for an independent, public-interest data
project that tracks WARN layoff notices; the records are not sought for any
commercial purpose.

My preferred format is a spreadsheet export (CSV or XLSX) including, where
available: employer name, worksite address/city/ZIP, notice date, layoff or
closure effective date, number of affected workers, and the closure/layoff type.
Copies of the underlying notice letters (PDF) are an acceptable alternative if no
tabular export exists.

Please deliver the records electronically to this email address. If fees would
apply, please send me an estimate before fulfilling the request — I am happy to
narrow the scope. If the Department declines, I would appreciate a written note to
that effect so I can pursue the request through a Tennessee co-requester.

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
