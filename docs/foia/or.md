**Subject:** Inquiry — availability of WARN Act notices older than six years

Dear Office of Workforce Investments,

Your WARN listing at ccwd.hecc.oregon.gov notes that HECC OWI retains WARN records
for six years. I am writing to ask whether WARN Act notices older than that
six-year window still exist in any form — for example in the State Archives, a
predecessor agency's records (CCWD), or the dataset published on data.oregon.gov —
and, if so, how I may request them under the Oregon Public Records Law (ORS
192.311 et seq.).

If older records do exist, please treat this message as a public records request
for all electronically available records of WARN notices received by the agency
prior to June 2020, preferably as a spreadsheet export (CSV or XLSX) including,
where available: employer name, worksite address/city/ZIP, notice date, layoff or
closure effective date, number of affected workers, and the closure/layoff type.
Copies of the underlying notice letters (PDF) are an acceptable alternative.

I am making this request on my own behalf for an independent, public-interest data
project that tracks WARN layoff notices; the records are not sought for any
commercial purpose.

Please deliver any records electronically to this email address. If fees would
exceed $25, please send me an estimate before fulfilling the request — I am happy
to narrow the scope.
