**Subject:** Public records request — historical WARN Act notices (non-commercial)

Dear Public Records Team,

Pursuant to the Arizona Public Records Law (A.R.S. §39-121 et seq.), I request
copies of all electronically available records of Worker Adjustment and Retraining
Notification (WARN) Act notices received by the Department's State Rapid Response
Coordinator / Workforce Solutions Administration, from the earliest electronically
available date through December 31, 2015. Notices from 2016 onward are available
through the Arizona Job Connection WARN search; this request covers the years
before that. A complete export through the present is equally welcome if that is
easier to produce.

As required by A.R.S. §39-121.01(D)(1), I state that this request is made for a
**non-commercial purpose**: I am requesting on my own behalf for an independent,
public-interest data project that tracks WARN layoff notices. The records are not
sought for sale, resale, or any other commercial use.

My preferred format is a spreadsheet export (CSV or XLSX) including, where
available: employer name, worksite address/city/ZIP, notice date, layoff or
closure effective date, number of affected workers, and the closure/layoff type.
Copies of the underlying notice letters (PDF) are an acceptable alternative if no
tabular export exists.

Please deliver the records electronically to this email address. If fees would
apply, please send me an estimate before fulfilling the request — I am happy to
narrow the scope. If any portion of this request is denied, please cite the
specific exemption relied upon.

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
