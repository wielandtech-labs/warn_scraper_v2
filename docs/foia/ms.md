**Subject:** Public records request — historical WARN Act notices

Dear Records Officer,

Pursuant to the Mississippi Public Records Act (Miss. Code Ann. §25-61-1 et seq.),
I request copies of all electronically available records of Worker Adjustment and
Retraining Notification (WARN) Act notices received by the Department of
Employment Security, from the earliest electronically available date through
program year 2019. The quarterly WARN reports from PY2020 onward published on
mdes.ms.gov are already in hand; this request covers the years before that. A
complete export through the present is equally welcome if that is easier to
produce.

I am making this request on my own behalf for an independent, public-interest data
project that tracks WARN layoff notices; the records are not sought for any
commercial purpose.

My preferred format is a spreadsheet export (CSV or XLSX) including, where
available: employer name, worksite address/city/ZIP, notice date, layoff or
closure effective date, number of affected workers, and the closure/layoff type.
Copies of the underlying notice letters or quarterly reports (PDF) are an
acceptable alternative if no tabular export exists.

Please deliver the records electronically to this email address. If fees would
exceed $25, please send me an estimate before fulfilling the request — I am happy
to narrow the scope. If any portion of this request is denied, please cite the
specific exemption relied upon.

Thank you for your time.
