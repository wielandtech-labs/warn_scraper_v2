Dear Information Disclosure Office,

Pursuant to the Government Records Access and Management Act (Utah Code §63G-2),
I request copies of all electronically available records of Worker Adjustment and
Retraining Notification (WARN) Act notices received by the Department of Workforce
Services, from the earliest electronically available date through December 31,
2025. A complete export through the present is equally welcome if that is easier
to produce.

I am making this request on my own behalf for an independent, public-interest data
project that tracks WARN layoff notices; the records are not sought for any
commercial purpose. On that basis I also request a fee waiver under Utah Code
§63G-2-203(4), as release of these records primarily benefits the public.

My preferred format is a spreadsheet export (CSV or XLSX) including, where
available: employer name, worksite address/city/ZIP, notice date, layoff or
closure effective date, number of affected workers, and the closure/layoff type.
Copies of the underlying notice letters (PDF) are an acceptable alternative if no
tabular export exists.

Please deliver the records electronically to this email address. If a fee waiver
is not granted and fees would exceed $25, please send me an estimate before
fulfilling the request — I am happy to narrow the scope. If any portion of this
request is denied, please cite the specific exemption relied upon.

Thank you for your time, and I look forward to your response within the timeframe
provided by GRAMA.
