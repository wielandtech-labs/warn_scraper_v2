Pursuant to the South Carolina Freedom of Information Act (S.C. Code Ann. §30-4-10
et seq.), I request copies of all electronically available records of Worker
Adjustment and Retraining Notification (WARN) Act notices received by the
Department of Employment and Workforce, from the earliest electronically available

I am making this request on my own behalf for an independent, public-interest data
project that tracks WARN layoff notices; the records are not sought for any
commercial purpose.

My preferred format is a spreadsheet export (CSV or XLSX) including, where
available: employer name, worksite address/city/ZIP, notice date, layoff or
closure effective date, number of affected workers, and the closure/layoff type.
Copies of the underlying notice letters (PDF) are an acceptable alternative if no
tabular export exists.

Please deliver the records electronically to this email address. If fees would
exceed $25, please send me an estimate before fulfilling the request — I am happy
to narrow the scope. If any portion of this request is denied, please cite the
specific exemption relied upon.

provided by the Act.
