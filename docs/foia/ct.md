**Subject:** FOIA request — historical WARN Act notices

**Body (for the portal request field):**

Pursuant to the Connecticut Freedom of Information Act (Conn. Gen. Stat. §1-200 et
seq.), I request copies of all electronically available records of Worker
Adjustment and Retraining Notification (WARN) Act notices received by the
Connecticut Department of Labor, from the earliest electronically available date
through December 31, 2018. WARN reports from 2019 onward are already published in
the Department's public document library; this request covers the years before
that. A complete export through the present is equally welcome if that is easier
to produce.

I am making this request on my own behalf for an independent, public-interest data
project that tracks WARN layoff notices; the records are not sought for any
commercial purpose.

My preferred format is a spreadsheet export (CSV or XLSX) including, where
available: employer name, worksite address/city/ZIP, notice date, layoff or
closure effective date, number of affected workers, and the closure/layoff type.
Copies of the underlying notice letters (PDF) are an acceptable alternative if no
tabular export exists.

Please deliver the records electronically to raphael.wieland@gmail.com. If fees
would exceed $25, please send me an estimate before fulfilling the request — I am
happy to narrow the scope. If any portion of this request is denied, please cite
the specific exemption relied upon.

Thank you for your time.
