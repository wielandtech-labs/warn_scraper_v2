**Subject:** FOIA request — historical WARN Act notices

Dear FOIA Coordinator,

Pursuant to the Delaware Freedom of Information Act (29 Del. C. ch. 100), I
request copies of all electronically available records of Worker Adjustment and
Retraining Notification (WARN) Act notices received by the Division of Employment
& Training / Dislocated Worker Unit, from the earliest electronically available
date through December 31, 2015. Notices from 2016 onward are available through the
Delaware JobLink WARN database; this request covers the years before that. A
complete export through the present is equally welcome if that is easier to
produce.

I am making this request on my own behalf for an independent, public-interest data
project that tracks WARN layoff notices; the records are not sought for any
commercial purpose.

My preferred format is a spreadsheet export (CSV or XLSX) including, where
available: employer name, worksite address/city/ZIP, notice date, layoff or
closure effective date, number of affected workers, and the closure/layoff type —
electronic delivery keeps the request within the no-cost page allowance. Copies of
the underlying notice letters (PDF) are an acceptable alternative if no tabular
export exists.

Please deliver the records electronically to this email address. If administrative
fees would apply, please send me an estimate before processing — I am happy to
narrow the scope. If any portion of this request is denied, please cite the
specific exemption relied upon.

Thank you for your time, and I look forward to your response within the fifteen
business days provided by §10003(h)(1).
