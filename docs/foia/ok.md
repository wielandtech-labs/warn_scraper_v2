**Subject:** Open Records Act request — WARN Act notices (all years)

Dear Custodian of Records,

Pursuant to the Oklahoma Open Records Act (51 O.S. §24A.1 et seq.), I request
copies of all electronically available records of Worker Adjustment and Retraining
Notification (WARN) Act notices received by the Oklahoma Employment Security
Commission, from the earliest electronically available date through the present.
The Commission's WARN listing on employoklahoma.gov requires a portal login and is
not publicly accessible, so this request covers all years.

I am making this request on my own behalf for an independent, public-interest data
project that tracks WARN layoff notices; the records are not sought for any
commercial purpose, and the request should not require a search fee under
51 O.S. §24A.5.

My preferred format is a spreadsheet export (CSV or XLSX) including, where
available: employer name, worksite address/city/ZIP, notice date, layoff or
closure effective date, number of affected workers, and the closure/layoff type —
electronic delivery avoids per-page copy fees. Copies of the underlying notice
letters (PDF) are an acceptable alternative if no tabular export exists.

Please deliver the records electronically to this email address, and let me know
an estimated completion date. If any fees would apply, please send me an estimate
before fulfilling the request — I am happy to narrow the scope. If any portion of
this request is denied, please cite the specific exemption relied upon.

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
Raphael Wieland
raphael.wieland@gmail.com
