- **To:** leo-warn@michigan.gov (the LEO WARN FAQ directs requests for notices no longer posted online to this address / FOIA)
- **Method:** email
- **Statute:** Michigan Freedom of Information Act, Act 442 of 1976 (MCL 15.231 et seq.)
- **Years sought:** earliest electronically available → Oct 2024 (michigan.gov removed pre-2025 notices from the public search; DB coverage starts 2024-11 — window widened 2026-07-02 per coverage-vs-aggregators.md)

**Subject:** FOIA request — historical WARN Act notices

Dear LEO WARN Office,

Pursuant to the Michigan Freedom of Information Act (Act 442 of 1976, MCL 15.231
et seq.), I request copies of all electronically available records of Worker
Adjustment and Retraining Notification (WARN) Act notices received by the
Department of Labor and Economic Opportunity, from the earliest electronically
available date through October 31, 2024. I understand from the Department's WARN
FAQ that notices older than those posted on michigan.gov must be requested rather
than downloaded — this is such a request. A complete export through the present is
equally welcome if that is easier to produce.

I am making this request on my own behalf for an independent, public-interest data
project that tracks WARN layoff notices; the records are not sought for any
commercial purpose. On that basis I also request a public-interest fee waiver
under MCL 15.234.

My preferred format is a spreadsheet export (CSV or XLSX) including, where
available: employer name, worksite address/city/ZIP, notice date, layoff or
closure effective date, number of affected workers, and the closure/layoff type.
Copies of the underlying notice letters (PDF) are an acceptable alternative if no
tabular export exists.

Please deliver the records electronically to this email address. If a fee waiver
is not granted and fees would exceed $25, please send me an estimate before
fulfilling the request — I am happy to narrow the scope. If any portion of this
request is denied, please cite the specific exemption relied upon.

Thank you for your time, and I look forward to your response within the five
business days provided by the Act.

Sincerely,
