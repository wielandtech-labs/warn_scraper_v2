**Subject:** Records Request — historical WARN Act notices (MPIA)

Dear Public Information Officer,

Pursuant to the Maryland Public Information Act (Md. Code, Gen. Provisions §4-101
et seq.), I request copies of all electronically available records of Worker
Adjustment and Retraining Notification (WARN) Act notices received by the Division
of Workforce Development and Adult Learning's Dislocation Services Unit, from the
earliest electronically available date through December 31, 2009. The Department's
published WARN log at labor.maryland.gov covers 2010 to the present; this request
covers the years before that. A complete export through the present is equally
welcome if that is easier to produce.

I am making this request on my own behalf for an independent, public-interest data
project that tracks WARN layoff notices; the records are not sought for any
commercial purpose. On that basis I also ask that any fees beyond the two free
hours provided by GP §4-206 be waived in the public interest.

My preferred format is a spreadsheet export (CSV or XLSX) including, where
available: employer name, worksite address/city/ZIP, notice date, layoff or
closure effective date, number of affected workers, and the closure/layoff type —
the same fields as the Department's published log. Copies of the underlying notice
letters (PDF) are an acceptable alternative if no tabular export exists.

Please deliver the records electronically to this email address. If a waiver is
not granted and fees would apply, please send me an estimate before processing — I
am happy to narrow the scope. If any portion of this request is denied, please
cite the specific exemption relied upon.

Thank you for your time.
