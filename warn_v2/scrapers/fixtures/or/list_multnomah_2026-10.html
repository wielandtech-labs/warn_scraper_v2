Enforcement of WARN requirements is through the United States district courts. While the Higher Education Coordinating Commission, Office of Workforce Investments (HECC, OWI) can provide guidance and information about the WARN Act, it does not issue formal interpretations of the law or the regulations. HECC may provide advice that represents its best judgment about WARNs, but it is not binding in the courts, and it does not replace the advice of an attorney. HECC OWI will only retain WARN records for six years.
