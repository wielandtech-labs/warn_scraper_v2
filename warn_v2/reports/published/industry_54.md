## Sentiment

Job losses in Professional, Scientific & Technical Services eased to 3,318 in the current 90-day window, down 41.4% from 5,661 in the prior window and 47.7% below the 6,342 recorded in the same window last year. Momentum and the seasonal comparison agree, and the long-run picture does too: trailing-12-month job losses of 18,135 are 27.6% below the 25,033 of the twelve months before. These figures cover only NAICS-enriched notices and are directional.

The easing is not shared everywhere. Washington worsened most, with job losses climbing to 791 from 265 (198.5%), and Michigan rose 82.8% to 479. Hawaii recorded 380, with no comparable activity in the prior window. Indiana eased the most, to 151 from 586, and Virginia declined 37.3% to 362.
