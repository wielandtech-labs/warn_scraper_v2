National WARN job losses eased 9.2% to 79,902 in the current 90-day window from 88,019 in the prior window, and fell 28.7% from 112,123 in the same window last year. Both comparisons point to relief, and trailing-12-month job losses of 368,524 are 18.2% below the 450,370 of the prior 12 months.

## Analysis

The national easing hides a sharp concentration shift. Washington's 17,321 job losses now nearly match California's, and its rise of 12,446 workers is the largest move among the states. Texas (up 36.1%) and Virginia (up 29.0%) also worsened, while New Jersey, Ohio and Michigan eased.

Sector mix has rotated. Transportation & Warehousing and Information, which led the prior window's losses, fell 80.9% and 78.2%. Health Care & Social Assistance, Wholesale Trade and Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing & Hunting rose, the last to 5,090 from 492.

The monthly path ran below the year-earlier month in most recent months, though 2026-01 and 2026-08 were above. The 2026-10 row is partial.
