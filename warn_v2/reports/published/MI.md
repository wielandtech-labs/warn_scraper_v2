## Sentiment

Job losses reported in Michigan WARN notices were nearly flat at 2,702 in the current 90-day window, down 2.7% from 2,778 in the prior window. Against the same window last year the picture is worse: job losses are 57.9% above the 1,711 recorded then. The long-run picture agrees with the seasonal one, with trailing-12-month job losses of 10,549, up 22.7% from 8,597.

Wayne recorded the most job losses at 643, down 13.2% from 741. A multi-county notice spanning Delta, Grand Traverse, Kent, Saginaw and Wayne accounted for 641, and Eaton and Ingham for 350, both where no comparable activity was recorded in the prior window. Oakland eased 64.3% to 234.

By sector, Wholesale Trade worsened, rising 254.1% to 641 from 181, and Health Care & Social Assistance recorded 557 where no comparable activity was recorded in the prior window. Manufacturing eased 44.5% to 306.

The model projects 968 job losses for 2026-10 (range 152–1,784) and 1,638 for 2026-12 (range 822–2,454).

## Analysis

Momentum is flat while the seasonal and 12-month comparisons both show job losses rising, so the elevated level has persisted rather than spiked. The monthly path is uneven: 2026-07 recorded 1,399 job losses against 555 a year earlier, 2026-08 eased to 518 against 1,160, and 2026-09 rose to 800 against 322.

Several of the largest current county rows are multi-county notices, which spread job losses across many areas and make county-level comparison less precise. Sector rows cover 91.7% of current-window notices.
