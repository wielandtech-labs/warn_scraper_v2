## Sentiment

Job losses in Transportation & Warehousing eased to 3,109 in the current 90-day window, down 77.1% from 13,575 in the prior window and 60.9% below the 7,946 recorded in the same window last year. Both comparisons agree, and trailing-12-month job losses of 28,982 are 14.8% below the 34,020 of the twelve months before.

A few states worsened against the trend. Texas now leads at 638, up 67.0%, Massachusetts rose 378.0% to 239, and Missouri recorded 227 with no comparable activity in the prior window. Florida eased 88.6% to 557 from 4,906.

By subsector, Air Transportation fell 98.2% to 143 from 7,819, accounting for much of the easing. Truck Transportation moved the other way, rising 418.3% to 425 from 82. Support Activities for Transportation eased 47.4% to 1,506.
