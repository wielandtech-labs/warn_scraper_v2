## Sentiment

Job losses in Manufacturing eased on both comparisons. The current window recorded 16,887 workers affected across 152 notices, down 6.5% from 18,056 in the prior window and 13.9% below the 19,613 recorded in the same window last year. The trailing 12 months (74,837) were 12.6% lower than the prior 12 months (85,591).

The easing is not uniform. California rose 58.1% to 3,886, Illinois climbed 286.6% to 2,745 and North Carolina rose 44.4% to 1,037, while New Jersey eased 11.1% to 777.

Food Manufacturing leads at 5,330, up 17.0%, and Computer and Electronic Product Manufacturing rose 94.2% to 2,696. Chemical Manufacturing eased 12.9% to 1,951.

BLS reports that Manufacturing payrolls fell early in the period covered and rose in most months since. These figures cover only NAICS-enriched notices and are directional.
