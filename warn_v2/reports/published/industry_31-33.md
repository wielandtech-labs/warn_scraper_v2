## Sentiment

Job losses in Manufacturing eased to 10,093 in the current 90-day window, down 44.4% from 18,146 in the prior window and 46.3% below the 18,797 recorded in the same window last year. Both comparisons agree, and the trailing-12-month total of 68,643 is 14.8% below the 80,559 of the twelve months before.

The easing is not universal. Ohio worsened sharply, with job losses climbing 228.0% to 1,919 from 585, and Georgia rose 225.7% to 544. Utah recorded 723 against 8 in the prior window. Pennsylvania eased 64.0% to 682.

By subsector, Transportation Equipment Manufacturing leads at 1,928, up 3.9%, while Chemical Manufacturing eased 39.1% to 1,597 and Food Manufacturing fell 69.1% to 1,489. Nonmetallic Mineral Product Manufacturing worsened, up 104.8% to 637.

BLS reports Manufacturing payrolls rose in every month from 2026-06 through 2026-09, by 9 thousand in 2026-09. These WARN figures cover only NAICS-enriched notices and are directional.

## Analysis

The headline relief is broad across horizons, but part of it rests on recent weeks: states post WARN notices with a lag, so the current window's total may rise as late filings arrive. Even allowing for that, each full month of the window ran below the same month last year: 5,007 against 6,873 in 2026-07, 3,979 against 6,065 in 2026-08, and 3,122 against 5,784 in 2026-09. The 2026-10 row is partial, so its 259 is not comparable with the 8,208 of a year earlier.

The pressure has moved geographically. Ohio's 1,919 job losses lead all states, and Ohio, Georgia and Utah all worsened while several larger prior-window states eased.

- Ohio, where job losses more than tripled against the prior window.
- Transportation Equipment Manufacturing, now the largest subsector and still rising slightly.
- Late filings revising the 2026-09 total.
