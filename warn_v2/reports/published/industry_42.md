## Sentiment

Job losses in Wholesale Trade eased to 2,698 in the current 90-day window, down 73.0% from 9,993 in the prior window and 59.2% below the 6,610 recorded in the same window last year. Both comparisons point the same way, and trailing-12-month job losses of 20,341 are 19.3% below the 25,204 of the twelve months before.

Several states moved against the trend. Michigan's job losses rose 254.1% to 641, Georgia's climbed 606.0% to 593, and Washington's rose 121.7% to 592. Texas eased sharply, down 83.2% to 347 from 2,064.

BLS reports Wholesale trade payrolls rose in every month from 2026-04 through 2026-09, by 5.0 thousand in 2026-09. These WARN figures cover only NAICS-enriched notices, so they are directional rather than complete.

## Analysis

The momentum comparison flatters the current window because the prior window included an unusually heavy 2026-04, with 7,222 job losses against 2,475 a year earlier. The year-on-year comparison, also sharply lower, is the steadier signal. States post WARN notices with a lag, so the recent weeks may rise as late filings arrive; 2026-09 shows just 303 job losses against 3,086 a year earlier, a gap late filings could narrow. The 2026-10 row is partial and not comparable with its year-earlier figure.
