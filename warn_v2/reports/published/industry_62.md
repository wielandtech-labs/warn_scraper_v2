## Sentiment

Job losses in Health Care & Social Assistance worsened against the prior window, rising 29.4% to 5,861 from 4,528, even as notices fell to 30 from 70. Against the same window last year the picture is better: job losses are 37.9% below the 9,444 recorded then. The long-run trend also shows relief, with trailing-12-month job losses of 21,694 sitting 11.0% below the 24,376 of the twelve months before. These figures cover only NAICS-enriched notices and are directional.

Massachusetts accounts for the most job losses, climbing to 1,554 from 542 (186.7%). Georgia (856) and Michigan (557) recorded no comparable activity in the prior window, and Virginia rose to 545 from 2. New York eased 21.3% to 280.

## Analysis

This is the sector where momentum and the seasonal comparison point in different directions, which fits its Grade C (stable) reading. Fewer notices are carrying more job losses, so the rise reflects larger filings rather than more of them.

The monthly path shows the turn. Job losses of 3,020 in 2026-08 ran above the 2,010 recorded a year earlier, while 2026-07 (1,554 against 4,455) and 2026-09 (1,807 against 2,243) stayed below. The 2026-10 row is partial.
